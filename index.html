About Me

I am Lee, Yuan Jyun (Jim), currently completing my B.S. degree in Applied Mathematics at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), while also pursuing a double major in Electronics and Electrical Engineering.

Motivated by problems arising from quantum theory, my research interests lie broadly in operator theory, quantum information theory, and noncommutative analysis. In particular, I am interested in quantum dynamical systems and the mathematical structures underlying quantum information, including operator systems, open quantum system, and entropy inequalities.

I am also interested in noncommutative probability, especially free probability, and its connections with finite-dimensional matrix analysis. More broadly, I hope to understand how tools from operator theory and noncommutative probability can be applied to problems in matrix analysis and quantum information theory.

I plan to apply to Ph.D. programs in Mathematics for Fall 2027.
