About Me

I am Lee Yuan Jyun (Jim), currently completing my B.S. degree in Applied Mathematics at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University. I am also pursuing a double major in the Department of Electronics and Electrical Engineering (EEE).

My research interests lie in operator theory and functional analysis, particularly in noncommutative analysis and its connections to ergodic theory and dynamical systems. I am especially interested in understanding long-term behavior through operator-algebraic and spectral methods.

Motivated by examples from quantum theory, I am interested in studying quantum dynamical systems, where noncommutative structures naturally arise. In this context, concepts such as quantum channels, open system dynamics, and quantum entropy provide rich settings in which ideas from noncommutative analysis and ergodic theory interact.\n I plan to apply to Ph.D. programs in Mathematics for Fall 2027. \n
