About Me

I am Lee Yuan Jyun (Jim), currently completing my B.S. degree in Applied Mathematics at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University. I am also pursuing a double major in the Department of Electronics and Electrical Engineering (EEE).

Motivated by examples from quantum theory, I am interested in studying quantum dynamics systems and quantum information theory, where noncommutative structures naturally arise. In this context, concepts such as CP maps, open system dynamics, and entropies provide settings in which ideas from noncommutative analysis and ergodic theory interact.

I plan to apply to Ph.D. programs in Mathematics for Fall 2027.

Research Interests

Quantum dynamics, operator algebras, noncommutative analysis, and their connections to ergodic theory and information theory.
